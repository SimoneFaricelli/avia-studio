Companies are investing in artificial intelligence, but introducing new tools is not enough.

Employees need to understand how AI can support their specific roles, responsibilities and everyday activities.

## Generic training has limitations

Traditional courses often provide the same content to every employee.

However, a marketing specialist, a financial analyst and an operations manager do not use artificial intelligence in the same way.

Effective AI training should consider:

- the employee's role;
- their current level of knowledge;
- their daily responsibilities;
- the objectives of the organization.

## From knowledge to application

Knowing what artificial intelligence is does not automatically help employees use it effectively.

Training becomes valuable when employees can connect what they learn to real activities, decisions and workflows.

## A more practical approach

Personalized training can help organizations develop relevant AI capabilities across different departments.

The goal is not simply to teach employees about artificial intelligence, but to help them apply it where it creates real business value.
